Our NYC Heritage

The Richtone Operating Philosophy

The Richtone Group LLC is a New York City-based construction company specializing in residential & commercial renovation projects, including both interior & exterior construction.

With a foundation in skilled carpentry and hands-on management, our company was built to meet the rigorous demands of New York’s institutional market — including universities, charter schools, and public landmarks.

By self-performing our own carpentry, framing, drywall, and door installations, we deliver renovations on schedule, inside occupied buildings, with one accountable team and one point of contact.
